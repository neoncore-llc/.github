## Common Questions

### How do I enable or customize features in-game?
Open the in-game menu using the assigned hotkey, navigate to the desired category (visual indicators, timers, or automation presets), toggle the feature on, and adjust the sliders or keybinds to match your preferences.

### How do I save or switch my configuration profile?
Open the profile manager tab in the menu, select a preset or create a new profile, and save your settings to sync them across sessions.
