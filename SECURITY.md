# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**NeonCore LLC** takes the security of our platform, user accounts, and web services seriously.

If you believe you have discovered a security issue across any NeonCore service or repository, please report it privately:

1. **Private Vulnerability Reporting:** Use the **Report a vulnerability** button under the **Security** tab of the relevant repository on GitHub.
2. **Direct Email:** Contact our security team at **[support@neoncore.llc](mailto:support@neoncore.llc)** with the subject line `[SECURITY] <Brief Summary>`.

### What to Include
- A clear description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Step-by-step instructions to reproduce the issue.
- Relevant URLs or screenshots demonstrating the finding.

### Response Timeline
- **Initial Acknowledgment:** Within 24 hours.
- **Triage & Assessment:** Within 72 hours.
- **Resolution & Remediation:** Prioritized by severity.

Please do not publicly disclose security issues before our team has confirmed and deployed a remediation.
